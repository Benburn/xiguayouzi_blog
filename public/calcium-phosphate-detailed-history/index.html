摘要 / Abstract

p.1 摘要
Original

Calcium phosphate (CaP) bioceramics are widely used in the field of bone regeneration, both in orthopedics and in dentistry, due to their good biocompatibility, osseointegration and osteoconduction. The aim of this article is to review the history, structure, properties and clinical applications of these materials, whether they are in the form of bone cements, paste, scaffolds, or coatings. Major analytical techniques for characterization of CaPs, in vitro and in vivo tests, and the requirements of the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and international standards from CaP coatings on orthopedic and dental endosseous implants, are also summarized, along with the possible effect of sterilization on these materials. CaP coating technologies are summarized, with a focus on electrochemical processes. Theories on the formation of transient precursor phases in biomineralization, the dissolution and reprecipitation as bone of CaPs are discussed. A wide variety of CaPs are presented, from the individual phases to nano-CaP, biphasic and triphasic CaP formulations, composite CaP coatings and cements, functionally graded materials (FGMs), and antibacterial CaPs. We conclude by foreseeing the future of CaPs.

中文

磷酸钙(CaP)生物陶瓷因其良好的生物相容性、骨整合(osseointegration)与骨传导(osteoconduction)能力,在骨科与牙科的骨再生领域被广泛使用。本文旨在综述这些材料的历史、结构、性质与临床应用,无论其形态为骨水泥、膏体、支架还是涂层。文中还总结了 CaP 表征的主要分析技术、体外与体内试验,以及美国食品药品监督管理局(FDA)与国际标准对骨科和牙科植入体 CaP 涂层的要求,并讨论了灭菌对这些材料可能产生的影响。CaP 涂层技术被系统归纳,重点放在电化学工艺上。文中讨论了生物矿化中瞬态前驱相形成的理论,以及 CaP 的溶解与再沉淀成骨机制;介绍了从单一物相到纳米 CaP、双相与三相 CaP 配方、复合 CaP 涂层与骨水泥、功能梯度材料(FGM)及抗菌 CaP 的多种体系。最后,我们对 CaP 的未来作出了展望。

1. Historical Perspective 历史视角

范围 / Pages: p.1–3

p.1 S003
Original

Calcium phosphate (CaP) is the common name of a family of minerals containing calcium cations (Ca²⁺) together with orthophosphate (PO₄³⁻), metaphosphate (PO₃⁻), or pyrophosphate (P₂O₇⁴⁻) anions, and sometimes hydrogen (H⁺) or hydroxide (OH⁻) ions. CaP is the principal form of calcium found in bovine milk and blood. It is the major inorganic constituent of bone (~60 wt % of bone), and the main constituent of tooth enamel (ca. 90%). Calcium phosphates with a Ca/P atomic ratio between 1.5 and 1.67 are called apatites (e.g., hydroxyapatite or fluorapatite). The term apatite was coined in 1786 by German geologist Abraham Gottlob Werner based on the ancient Greek word "apatao", which means "to mislead".

中文

磷酸钙(CaP)是一族矿物的通称,由钙阳离子(Ca²⁺)与正磷酸根(PO₄³⁻)、偏磷酸根(PO₃⁻)或焦磷酸根(P₂O₇⁴⁻)阴离子组成,有时还含有氢离子(H⁺)或氢氧根离子(OH⁻)。CaP 是牛乳和血液中钙的主要存在形式;它是骨的主要无机成分(约占骨质量的 60%),也是牙釉质的主要成分(约 90%)。Ca/P 原子比在 1.5 至 1.67 之间的磷酸钙称为磷灰石(如羟基磷灰石或氟磷灰石)。"磷灰石"(apatite)一词由德国地质学家 Abraham Gottlob Werner 于 1786 年根据古希腊词 "apatao"("欺骗、误导")创造。

p.1 S004
Original

The history of CaPs has been reviewed in various articles. The first articles to describe the structure and composition of bones, teeth and other types of calcified tissues appeared in the last quarter of the 17th century. In 1769, the Swedish chemist and metallurgist Johan Gottlieb Gahn discovered the existence of CaP in bones. However, only since this fact was reported by the German-Swedish pharmaceutical chemist Carl Wilhelm Scheele in 1771, has phosphorus been produced from bone ash. In the last decade of the 18th century, the French chemists Antoine François Comte de Fourcroy and Nicolas Louis Vauquelin discovered the existence of acidic CaP, nowadays known as monocalcium phosphate monohydrate (MCPM), monocalcium phosphate anhydrous (MCPA), dicalcium phosphate anhydrous (DCPA, monetite), and dicalcium phosphate dihydrate (DCPD, brushite). The organic-inorganic composite nature of bone has been known since at least 1788.

中文

CaP 的历史已在多篇文章中综述。最早描述骨、牙及其他钙化组织结构与成分的文章出现于 17 世纪最后 25 年。1769 年,瑞典化学家兼冶金学家 Johan Gottlieb Gahn 发现了骨中存在 CaP;但直到 1771 年德裔瑞典制药化学家 Carl Wilhelm Scheele 报道此事后,人们才开始从骨灰中制备磷。18 世纪最后 10 年,法国化学家 Antoine François Comte de Fourcroy 与 Nicolas Louis Vauquelin 发现了酸性磷酸钙的存在,即今天所称的一水磷酸二氢钙(MCPM)、无水磷酸二氢钙(MCPA)、无水磷酸氢钙(DCPA,monetite 三斜磷钙石)与二水磷酸氢钙(DCPD,brushite 透磷钙石)。骨具有有机–无机复合本质这一认识至少可追溯至 1788 年。

p.2 S005
Original

Various processes for preparation of calcium-deficient hydroxyapatite (CDHA) were developed by 1807. In 1808, Nicholson already reported the considerable compositional differences between enamel, dentin, and bones. In 1969, hot-pressed HAp powder into dense and useful shapes was reported. This may be the earliest article on the fabrication of CaP implants. In 1971, the first study on preparation of biodegradable porous β-TCP scaffolds was published. In 1973, the first study on preparation and implantation of resorbable and porous CaP (specifically, β-TCP) was published. In 1975, the modern dental application of CaP began: β-TCP was applied both in surgically created periodontal defects and as an adjunct to apical closure in pulpless permanent teeth. In 1979, dense HAp cylinders were used for immediate tooth root replacement.

中文

到 1807 年,已发展出多种制备缺钙羟基磷灰石(CDHA)的工艺。1808 年,Nicholson 便已报道牙釉质、牙本质与骨之间成分上的显著差异。1969 年有报道将热压 HAp 粉末制成致密且有用的形状——这可能是关于 CaP 植入体制备的最早文章。1971 年发表了首项关于制备可生物降解多孔 β-TCP 支架的研究;1973 年发表了首项关于可吸收多孔 CaP(具体为 β-TCP)制备与植入的研究。1975 年,CaP 的现代牙科应用开始:β-TCP 被用于外科创建的牙周缺损,并作为无髓恒牙根尖封闭的辅助材料。1979 年,致密 HAp 圆柱体被用于即刻牙根替代。

On the macroscopic level, bones can have diverse shapes depending on their respective function. Yet, bones are usually categorized into two types: cortical (or compact) bone, and cancellous (or trabecular) bone, see Figure 1b. Cortical bone forms the outer shell of most bones. These can reach a thickness of between several tenths of a millimetre (in vertebra) to several millimetres or even centimetres (in the mid-shaft of long bones). It is a fairly dense bone, with porosity in the order of 6%. Cancellous bone usually forms inside of bones that are under compressive stress. The interconnecting framework of trabeculae is in a number of combinations, all following basic cellular structures: rod-rod, rod-plate, or plate-plate. Trabecular bone has pores in the order of 80%. 3. Transient Precursor Phases 瞬态前驱相

范围 / Pages: p.8–11

p.8 S011
Original

Transient amorphous mineral phases have been detected in biomineral systems in different phyla of the animal kingdom. A poorly ordered hydrated iron oxide phase, called ferrihydrite, was identified as a transient precursor mineral phase in the biomineralization forming the tooth of the chiton. After a few days, the ferrihydrite transforms into crystalline magnetite. The formation of the skeleton of sea urchin made of calcite has been reported to begin with the initial deposition of amorphous calcium carbonate. The presence of an abundant ACP phase has also been demonstrated in the newly formed zebrafish fin bony rays. While the structure of bone is reasonably well defined, the process of the bone mineral formation remains controversial. In solutions, ACP is readily converted to stable crystalline phases such as OCP or apatitic products. One biomineralization strategy that has received significant attention is mineralization via transient precursor phases.

中文

在动物界不同门类的生物矿化体系中,均已检测到瞬态无定形矿物相。一种有序度差的水合氧化铁相——水铁矿(ferrihydrite)——被鉴定为石鳖牙齿生物矿化中的瞬态前驱矿物相,数天后水铁矿转变为结晶磁铁矿。据报道,海胆方解石骨骼的形成始于无定形碳酸钙的初始沉积;斑马鱼新生鳍条骨中也已证实存在大量的 ACP 相。尽管骨的结构已相当清楚,骨矿物的形成过程仍存争议。在溶液中,ACP 很容易转变为 OCP 或磷灰石类产物等稳定晶相。一种备受关注的生物矿化策略便是"经由瞬态前驱相矿化"。

p.8 S012
Original

Discussion about the possible precursor of apatite in bones arose in the 1970s, after the discovery of a precursor phase in vitro. In the 1960s and the 1970s it was already proposed that ACP serves as a precursor to HAp in vivo. It was argued that the initial ACP precursor undergoes a solid-state transformation to poorly crystalline apatite. Alternatively, it was suggested that the amorphous material observed in early mineralization is a paracrystalline mineral, i.e., it shows loss of long-range order as a result of lattice imperfections. In 1972 it was shown that the first-formed phase in vitro is ACP. This subsequently transforms into OCP, and finally into carbonate apatite.

中文

关于骨中磷灰石可能前驱相的讨论始于 20 世纪 70 年代,此前已在体外发现了前驱相。60–70 年代即有学者提出 ACP 在体内作为 HAp 的前驱体:初始 ACP 前驱体经固态转变成为弱结晶磷灰石;另一种观点认为,早期矿化中观察到的无定形物质是一种"次晶"(paracrystalline)矿物,即因晶格缺陷而丧失长程有序。1972 年证实体外最先形成的相是 ACP,它随后转变为 OCP,最终变为碳酸磷灰石。

4. Dissolution and Reprecipitation as Bone 溶解与再沉淀成骨

范围 / Pages: p.11–13

p.10 S013
Original

The supersaturation of different CaP phases in solution is dependent on many parameters, such as the calcium and phosphate ion concentrations in solution, pH and temperature. In order to determine which phases are most likely to precipitate in a specific system, one should first examine which phases are thermodynamically possible. To this aim, dedicated software such as ChemEQL, AQUASIM, and PHREEQC is available and has been applied in studies of both CaP precipitation from solution and electrochemical deposition of CaP coatings. PHREEQC not only can supply the answer to which phase is more supersaturated in solution, but also provides information on the ionic strength I and free ion activities product (IAP) with respect to different CaP phases.

17. Conclusions 结论

Original

Calcium phosphate (CaP) bioceramics are widely used in the field of bone regeneration, both in orthopedics and in dentistry, due to their good biocompatibility, osseointegration and osteoconduction. This article reviews many aspects of CaP bioceramics, in the form of bone cements, paste, scaffolds, and coatings. It starts with a brief historical perspective of their development and uses. Next, it describes the structure, chemistry and mechanical properties of bone. Then, it describes the transient precursor phases that have been observed, both in vitro and in vivo, and the mechanisms of dissolution of CaPs and their reprecipitation as bone. Next, the key requirements from CaPs for medical applications are presented. The structure, properties and applications of CaP-based materials are reviewed, starting with the individual phases as a function of their Ca-to-P atomic ratio, then nano-CaP, biphasic and triphasic formulations, composites and functionally graded materials (FGMs). The antibacterial behaviour of CaPs thanks to incorporation of certain antibacterial agents and the effect of different sterilization processes on CaPs are reviewed. Next, key principles of in vitro and in vivo tests are described. A variety of technologies for coating CaPs are reviewed, focusing on electrochemical processes and the requirements of the US FDA and international standards. Next, a variety of commercial products is listed according to their composition, and major applications are visualized. Finally, key issues in future developments are listed, along with recent developments in additive manufacturing (AM) of CaPs and hyperelastic bone.
